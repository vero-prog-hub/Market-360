# Poorna Market

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, A-Frame 1.5.0 and its bundled Three.js renderer. No backend or checkout.

## Product purpose
Let visitors explore an artistic, modern interpretation of Poorna Market in Visakhapatnam through a first-person street walk and a guided shop tour, on desktop, mobile web, or compatible WebXR headsets.

## Confirmed requirements
Semi-realistic modern Indian street architecture with traditional cultural character. Flower borders and selected petal carpets. Six G+2 buildings. Sixteen distinct local shops, bilingual Telugu/English signs, entrance arch and terminal shrine. Preserve the plum/gold interface and golden-hour light.

The surrounding neighborhood contains scenic residential/shop blocks, adjoining roads, footpaths and trees so the market sits within a town. A persistent Day/Evening toggle changes the scene sky, haze, daylight, illuminated windows and lamps while preserving the interface. The adjoining roads are background scenery; the existing market walking envelope and tour remain the interactive route.

## Exploration
Drag to look; keyboard walking; ground gliding; selectable shopfronts and wraparound tour. Keyboard-readable descriptions, reduced motion, accessible Help, sound and volume controls. Audio failure must never block exploration.

## Assets and sound
Bundle runtime assets locally. The approved Pixabay track is inaccessible behind a browser challenge. User explicitly approved an original locally generated tabla-and-flute-style loop as the substitute on 1 November 2026. Identify it honestly as an original instrument-inspired composition, not a recording of live performers.

## Boundaries
An artistic modernization, not a measured reconstruction or certified building-code proposal. No checkout, interior exploration, moving traffic, animated crowds, deployment, or remote pushes in this pass.
